Growth Opportunities

Keyword Opportunities

How to reach more people looking for what The SSam actually serves

1. Make “ssam” a keyword

The single word “ssam” produced 7 of the 16 orders in this report, more than any other search. It isn't a keyword in the campaign today; Google has only been showing the ad for it by loosely matching other keywords. Adding it, plus the dish-name searches below, guarantees the ad is eligible every time. People searching the dish who haven't heard of The SSam yet are exactly the new customers to reach.

2. Use “lettuce wraps” in the ads, not as keywords

Few people search for wraps near the restaurant. Since Aug 10, every wrap-related search combined (“lettuce wraps near me,” “korean lettuce wraps,” “ssam wraps” and similar) showed the ad about 11 times with no clicks. The existing keywords “ssam wraps nyc” and “low carb wraps nyc” were never shown at all. The better move is to put “Korean lettuce wraps” in the ad headlines and sitelinks. Then a guest who doesn't know the word “ssam” instantly understands what The SSam serves.

3. Retire keywords that attract the wrong guests

A few current keywords bring in searches for food The SSam doesn't make. Pausing them goes hand in hand with the negative keywords above.
